    Getting there

    Built around Arcus, not the other way round

    A dedicated implementation consultant maps your workflow and builds BigChange to fit it. Roll-out is usually eight to ten weeks, and you don’t pay until it’s running.

    1
    Weeks 1–4 · Discovery & build

    Your process, mapped

    • 15–20 hours one-to-one with a consultant who’s set up thousands of customers.
    • Job stages, survey and install worksheets, RAMS and stage-invoice automations built for Arcus.
    • Customers, vans and fitters loaded, and Xero linked.

    Tip: turn up on day one with your process on a whiteboard.

    2
    Pilot

    Two fitters first

    • Start with the fitter most likely to love it and the one most likely to push back.
    • Between them, most of the creases get ironed out.
    • Office runs a live job end to end before everyone switches.
    3
    Weeks 8–10 · Roll-out

    Everyone live, then build on it

    • Whole field team and subbies on the app.
    • WhatsApp groups, Google Calendar and Excel job sheets retired.
    • Later, if you want: move Capsule over, or link Quotient through the open API.
